Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

Velo is a minimal study timer and assignment tracker built for students. We believe in transparency: all your personal task and study data stays on your device, while standard web analytics and ad networks help keep the service free.

Your Task & Study Data

Your assignments, timers, and focus logs stay on your device. Velo stores all tasks, calendar deadlines, focus sessions, and settings in your browser's Local Storage. This data is never uploaded to any remote backend server operated by Velo.

Analytics & Session Insights

To understand general usage patterns, optimize interface performance, and fix bugs, Velo uses:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — collects aggregated, anonymous usage events and traffic metrics.
  • Microsoft Clarity — captures anonymous user interactions and session heatmaps to help identify UI friction and usability issues.

Advertising

Velo is free to use and supported by advertising via Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Cookies & Tracking

Velo does not set first-party cookies for study data. However, third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) and advertising partners (Google AdSense) may set cookies and similar tracking technologies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage & Control

  • All user assignments, session notes, and timer histories remain in your browser's local storage.
  • Clearing your browser cache and local storage erases your local Velo data.

Children's Privacy

Velo does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children or students.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will always be reflected on this page with a refreshed date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
